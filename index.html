About

Curious about data, careful about claims.

I'm Yibo Sun (孙弋博), an undergraduate at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, studying Data Science and Big Data Technology with Contemporary Entrepreneurialism. My work sits on the data side of generative AI: producing training data end to end, and then making sure every judgment about a generated image is one I can defend.

In a recent internship I generated target images with an AI agent, compared them against reference originals across structure, detail and style, and graded each pair for similarity and deviation. What I enjoyed most was the review: finding edge cases, turning them into clearer annotation rules, and keeping quality consistent across a batch — an execute, retrospect, iterate loop.

This site is my own experiment in the same spirit — a hand-built page, no frameworks, just HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript.
